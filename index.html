Meet CeCe!

More than just a coin, CeCe is the heart (and a little bit of the currency) of Commercium. She believes every great venture starts with a single idea... and maybe a little courage, too.

Curious, optimistic, and always one step ahead, CeCe is on a mission to turn ideas into opportunities, questions into discoveries, and ambition into action.
